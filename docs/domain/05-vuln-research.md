# Domain 05 — Vulnerability Research

Blueprint-aligned scope: CVE enrichment, exploit references, private vulnerability tracking, PoC metadata, vulnerability knowledge and patch analysis.

## Implemented contract
- CVE and advisory references are stored as intelligence, not blindly executed.
- PoC artifacts are isolated and approval-gated.
- Patch analysis captures affected versions, root cause and remediation.
- Research evidence is normalized and linked to findings and reports.

## Safety boundary
Exploit-development material is handled as controlled research artifacts. No default workflow targets third-party systems or bypasses the platform's authorization gates.
