# Domain 14 — Governance & Audit

Blueprint V4 implementation contract.

## Modules
- Policy Engine: scope, approval and data policy.
- Approval Workflow: multi-level approval and escalation.
- Audit Log: immutable, tamper-evident chain.
- Compliance Mapping: SOC2, ISO27001, PCI-DSS and GDPR mapping metadata.
- Data Retention: archive, deletion and legal-hold state.
- Incident Response: breach response, containment and recovery workflow.

Governance is a prerequisite to high-risk execution; missing or ambiguous authorization fails closed.
