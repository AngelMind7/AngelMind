# Domain 12 — Threat Intelligence

Blueprint-aligned scope: CVE/IOC feeds, threat-actor mapping, brand mentions, source tracking and intelligence enrichment.

## Implemented contract
- Intelligence sources are provenance-tagged.
- IOCs support normalized indicators and confidence.
- Threat-actor references map to defensive techniques and observed signals.
- External feeds are provider-gated and rate-limit aware.
- Collection is bounded by legal, contractual and workspace policy.
